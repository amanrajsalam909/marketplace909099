Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information RajkotMarket ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, phone number, email and saved delivery addresses.
  • Order information — items purchased, payments, returns, exchanges and complaints.
  • Refund details — UPI ID or bank account you choose to save, used only to pay you refunds.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical data (such as browser type) needed to run the site securely.

2. Voice calls (internet calling)

When you place or answer an in-app voice call (for example, with a delivery or return-pickup partner):

  • The call is a direct internet (peer-to-peer) voice call between the two devices. It does not use the public phone network.
  • We use your microphone and the network connection details needed to set up the call.
  • Your phone number is never shared with the other person — calls are connected without revealing it.
  • Calls are not recorded.
  • We ask for your consent once before your first call and remember your choice on your device.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and process your orders.
  • To arrange delivery, returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To let you and our partners contact each other about an order.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

4. Sharing

We share only what is necessary — for example, your delivery address with the vendor and assigned delivery partner so your order can reach you. We do not sell your personal data. Where we use service providers (such as a licensed telecom provider for masked calling, or cloud hosting), they process data only on our instructions.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent you have given. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and accounting requirements, after which it is deleted or archived securely.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@rajkotmarket.in.
