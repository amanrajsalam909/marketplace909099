## Does This Apply to RajkotMarket?

RajkotMarket is an **intermediary** under Section 2(1)(w) of the IT Act 2000 (it hosts and transmits third-party information — vendor products and customer orders). Rule 3(1) applies to **all intermediaries**. Rule 4 (chief compliance officer, nodal officer) applies only to **Significant Social Media Intermediaries (SSMIs)** — platforms with 5 million+ registered users. RajkotMarket is NOT an SSMI and Rule 4 does not apply.

**Applicable rules: Rule 3(1) and Rule 3(2) only.** All Rule 3 obligations are now addressed.
