# RajkotMarket Return & Exchange Policy
### (Adapted from AJIO framework — customised for 40–50 min local delivery)

---

## Important: Request Deadline for Fast-Delivery Orders

> RajkotMarket delivers in **40–50 minutes**. Because items are delivered so quickly, return and exchange requests **must be raised within 2–3 hours of delivery**. Once this window passes, the order is considered accepted.

This applies to all reasons — wrong item, size issue, damaged, change of mind — everything. Check your order immediately upon delivery.

---

## Return Window (after request is approved)

- **20 days** from delivery date to complete the return handover (once the request has been accepted)
- Request must still be initiated within **2–3 hours of delivery** (see above)
- Items must be in original condition when handed over during pickup

---

## Valid Reasons to Return

Only the following reasons are accepted. Requests citing other reasons will not be processed.

### Product / Quality Issues
1. **Wrong item delivered** — item received is different from what was ordered (different product, brand, or variant)
2. **Wrong size or colour delivered** — correct product but incorrect size or colour compared to the order
3. **Item is damaged or broken** — physical damage visible at the time of opening (torn, cracked, defective)
4. **Item is defective or not functioning** — product does not work as described (electronics, appliances, gadgets)
5. **Item is expired** — food, cosmetics, or consumables past their expiry date
6. **Counterfeit or not as described** — item appears fake or significantly different from the product listing photos/description
7. **Missing parts or accessories** — item delivered incomplete (missing belt, charger, buttons, etc.)
8. **Stained or soiled on arrival** — item received with visible stains, marks, or odour before use

### Size & Fit Issues (Exchange Only)
9. **Size too small** — ordered correct size but item runs smaller than standard sizing
10. **Size too large** — ordered correct size but item runs larger than standard sizing
11. **Fit not as expected** — silhouette, cut, or length significantly different from product description

### Change of Mind (Return Only — within 2–3 hour window)
12. **No longer needed** — customer changed their mind before or immediately after delivery
13. **Ordered by mistake** — duplicate order or incorrect product selected at checkout

---

## Valid Reasons to Exchange

Exchange is available for reasons **1, 2, 9, 10, 11** from the list above.

- Exchange is for **different size or colour of the same product only** (subject to availability)
- Only **one exchange** per order item is permitted
- If the exchanged item is also unsatisfactory for a valid reason, a return + refund may be raised on that item

---

## Non-Returnable / Non-Exchangeable Items

The following are **final sale** — no return, no exchange under any circumstance:

### Innerwear & Hygiene
- Briefs, boxers, shapewear bottoms
- Socks and hosiery
- Lingerie sets that include briefs

### Personal Care & Cosmetics
- Deodorants and perfumes
- Lip balms, makeup, and grooming products — **especially if the safety seal is broken**

### Customised Items
- Any personalised or altered clothing and accessories tailored specifically for you (custom embroidery, monograms, alterations, custom fits)

### Clearance & Special Sale Items
- Products explicitly marked **"Non-Returnable"** during flash sales or clearance events
- Sale tags on such items will clearly indicate this at the time of purchase

---

## Eligibility Conditions

All returned items must meet the following:

1. Original tags and labels intact (not removed or tampered)
2. Unworn, unwashed, and unaltered
3. Original packaging present
4. Return request raised within **2–3 hours of delivery**
5. No signs of use beyond opening and inspecting

---

## Return Pickup Process

1. Raise return/exchange request within **2–3 hours of delivery** via RajkotMarket
2. Request reviewed and approved (or rejected with reason) by the vendor or admin
3. **Free reverse pickup** scheduled — our delivery partner visits your address
4. Pickup within **3–5 business days** of request approval
5. Hand over the item in original packaging with tags attached
6. Collect and save the pickup acknowledgement

---

## Refund Policy

| Payment Mode | Refund Destination | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Online (UPI / card / netbanking) | Back to original payment source | 6–9 business days after pickup |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) | 6–9 business days after pickup |

- Refund is processed **only after** the returned item is received and passes quality check
- If the item fails QC (used, tags removed, damaged by customer), it will be returned to you and no refund will be issued
- **Bank account details (IFSC + account number) required for COD refunds** — enter in your profile before raising a request

---

## Damaged / Wrong Item Received

- Report within **48 hours of delivery** with clear photographs of the item and packaging
- RajkotMarket arranges free pickup and full refund or replacement at no cost
- No deduction is made when the error is on our side

> Tip: Always photograph the sealed package before opening. This protects you in case of a dispute.

---

## Cancellation Policy

- Orders can be cancelled **before the vendor accepts** — full refund immediately
- Once the vendor accepts and the item is being prepared / dispatched, cancellation is not possible
- Refund for pre-dispatch cancellation: credited back within 2–5 business days
